01 The holdfast

Kelp has no roots. It holds on anyway.

A strand of kelp grips bare rock with a holdfast: a clenched knot of fingers that grows into every crack and spreads the pull of each wave across hundreds of small contact points. Winter storms roll boulders along the sea floor. The kelp stays.

We studied the things that refuse to slip, then put them underfoot. Four animals, four soles, one rule for every shoe we make.

Grip first. Everything else second.
