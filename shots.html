Holdfast shot sheet

Every image, scroll film and 3D model the site uses, with the exact prompt. Until each exists the site shows a designed stand-in: a labelled placeholder, a storyboard that plays as you scroll, or a 3D shoe drawn in code. Generating them is the only step left.

Automatic (Higgsfield CLI)

  1. See the plan, cost and balance: node higgsfield/generate.mjs
  2. Generate everything: node higgsfield/generate.mjs --go
  3. Reload the site. Images appear in their slots.

By hand (Higgsfield app)

  1. Make the four stage 1 -side masters first. They define each shoe.
  2. For each shot: copy the prompt, pick the model and aspect shown, and for stage 2 attach the master named under “Reference”.
  3. Download, save as higgsfield/renders/<id>.png, then run node higgsfield/optimize.mjs.
